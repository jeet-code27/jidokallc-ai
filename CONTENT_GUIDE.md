## 2. Dual Audience Segmentation (For Home Page Toggle)

### A. Boutique / Small Business Track
- **Focus**: Local businesses, clinics, contractors, retail boutiques, gym owners, service providers.
- **Pain Points**: Missed calls after hours, timecards on paper, manual inventory updates, lead follow-up delays, lack of dedicated tech staff.
- **Offerings & Deliverables**:
  - After-hours voice receptionist & missed call text-back
  - Simple 2-way scheduling & reminder cascades
  - Lightweight CRM auto-tagging & quote follow-up
  - Fast turnaround (2–4 weeks), accessible ROI, DWY (Done With You) or DFY (Done For You).

### B. Enterprise Track
- **Focus**: Mid-to-large operations, multi-location companies, high-volume service dispatch, multi-agent architectures.
- **Pain Points**: Complex cross-system data silos, compliance & audit trails, custom LLM integration, high-concurrency voice/omnichannel routing, legacy system bridging.
- **Offerings & Deliverables**:
  - Full-scale multi-agent orchestration
  - HIPAA-compliant medical intake & secure document parsing (PDFs/Invoices/Contracts)
  - Custom API middleware & ERP/CRM deep synchronization
  - Dedicated SLA, enterprise security, named lead engineers, bespoke infrastructure.

## 4. Delivery Models / Approach Tiers
- **DIY (Do It Yourself - Self-Directed)**: Workflow mapping, build blueprint, architecture doc, and guidance calls. Best for internal tech teams.
- **DWY (Done With You - Collaborative [Most Popular])**: Core system built by Jidoka, team trained to operate and extend it, 30 days support.
- **DFY (Done For You - Managed Studio)**: Full end-to-end custom build, proactive monitoring, continuous maintenance, named engineer.
